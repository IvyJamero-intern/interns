# Outreach Sequence — AI Ambassador (msg2ai Events)

**Goal:** Book discovery calls / demos with decision-makers at Philippine venues, MICE/PCO organizers, and corporate events teams.

**Product:** [AI Ambassador](https://github.com/RethinkLedgers/msg2ai-leads) — a WhatsApp/SMS/AI concierge for event organizers that handles attendee onboarding, agenda, meeting requests, and QR check-in.

---

## Outreach Cadence (3-Touch Email)

| Touch | Timing | Purpose | CTA |
|-------|--------|---------|-----|
| 1 | Day 0 | Value + Hook (personalized) | "15-min call to see a live demo" |
| 2 | +5 days | Case study / Social proof | "See how [similar org] uses it" |
| 3 | +10 days | Break-up | "Worth one last look?" |
